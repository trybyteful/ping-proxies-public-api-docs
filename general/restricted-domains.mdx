title: 'Restricted Domains & Ports'
description: 'Understanding domain and port restrictions in the Byteful network'
---

Byteful blocks some domains and some kinds of traffic to keep the network secure, prevent abuse, and stay compliant. As an IWF (Internet Watch Foundation) member, we also enforce some of their blocklists.

## Blocked by Default

Some traffic is blocked by default because it carries a higher risk of abuse. If your use case requires it and you have a legitimate business reason, you can request access. We review requests case by case. AI inference access requires KYC verification. Direct IP access typically requires it too.

| Traffic | Blocked from | How to request access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct IP addresses (requests that target an IP address instead of a hostname) | 7 September 2026 | Open a ticket in your dashboard with a short explanation of what you need it for. |
| AI inference APIs: `api.openai.com` and `api.anthropic.com` | 30 October 2026 | Email [support@byteful.com](mailto:support@byteful.com) with a short explanation of what you need it for. Only available to customers based outside Hong Kong and China. |

## What We Restrict

- **Government websites** (`.gov`, `.mil` and country-specific government domains)
- **Banking, financial services and payment processors** (banks, PayPal, Stripe, etc.)
- **NSFW websites**
- **Mail services** (national postal services)
- **Select e-commerce platforms** (case by case)
- **Direct IP addresses** and **AI inference APIs** (see above)

Our full blocklist contains **over 6,000 items and grows every day**. Many entries are entire top-level domains: blocking `gov` restricts millions of websites in one rule. The examples below are **indicative, not exhaustive**.

<Warning>
  We do not publish or share our full blocklist. Keeping it private is a deliberate part of how we prevent fraud and abuse. To check whether a specific domain is accessible for a legitimate use case, contact [support@byteful.com](mailto:support@byteful.com).
</Warning>

## What You See When a Domain Is Blocked

Requests to a restricted domain can fail with a connection failure, an access denied error, a timeout, or an HTTP 403 error. See [Debugging and Error Codes](/general/debugging-and-error-codes) for the meaning of each code.

## Requesting Exceptions

If a legitimate business need requires access to a blocked domain, email [support@byteful.com](mailto:support@byteful.com) with the domain and your use case. We review each request case by case. We rarely grant exceptions for government, banking and financial domains.

## Reporting Domains That Should Be Blocked

If you are affiliated with an organisation that should be shielded from proxy traffic, such as an operator of essential or public-interest services, email [compliance@byteful.com](mailto:compliance@byteful.com). Include the domain, your connection to it, and why it needs protection. We may ask you to confirm your affiliation. We cannot restrict commercial websites only because they carry valuable public data.

See our [Acceptable Usage Policy](/general/acceptable-usage-policy) for more on our network guidelines.

## Restricted Ports

We keep an internal list of ports under risk of abuse, and we block all traffic to these ports with no exceptions. The list includes ports for services such as email and SSH. To check whether a port that your use case needs is blocked, please contact customer support at [support@byteful.com](mailto:support@byteful.com).
