title: "Converting a Personal Workspace"
description: "Learn how to convert your personal workspace into an organization, and what happens to your services, billing and API keys"
---

You can convert your personal workspace into an organization. Your active services, billing and API keys move into the organization, so your team can work with them.

<Warning>
  Conversion cannot be undone. Services and billing cannot be moved back to a personal account after converting.
</Warning>

## What Happens When You Convert

- **Proxies and services move over.** All active services transfer to the organization workspace.
- **Billing moves with them.** Future invoices are issued under the organization.
- **API keys stay valid.** All existing keys keep working, and you do not need to change your integrations.
- **Your personal account stays separate.** Byteful creates a new, empty personal workspace for you, so you can switch between personal and organization at any time.
- **You are the owner.** You can transfer ownership to another member later.

## Converting Your Workspace

1. Make sure that you are in your personal workspace. The workspace switcher at the bottom of the sidebar shows **Personal**.
2. Open the workspace switcher and click **User settings**.
3. On the **General** tab, find the **Convert to organization** banner and click **Convert account**.

<img
  src="/images/general/organizations/convert-banner.png"
  alt="Byteful Convert to Organization"
/>

When the conversion is complete, the workspace switcher shows your new organization and a new **Personal** workspace.

The limit on the number of organizations that you can own also applies to conversions. See [Creating an Organization](/general/organizations/creating-an-organization).
