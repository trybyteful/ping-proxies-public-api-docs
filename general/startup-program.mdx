The Byteful Startup Program gives qualifying startups up to 50% off and free residential data. It is open to bootstrapped startups, startups backed by a VC fund or an accelerator, and Y Combinator companies.

## How it works

1. Choose the track that matches your startup.
2. Apply with the form for that track.
3. Our team reviews your application and contacts you with the result.
