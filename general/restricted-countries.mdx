---
title: "Restricted Countries"
description: "Countries whose residents cannot use Byteful services"
---

Byteful does not do business with residents of the following countries:

- Afghanistan
- Belarus
- Cuba
- Iraq
- Iran
- North Korea
- Myanmar
- Palestine
- Russia
- Syria
- Yemen

This restriction applies to where the customer resides, not to their citizenship. A citizen of one of these countries who is a legal resident in a non-restricted country will not be restricted from using Byteful services. However, all residents of those countries will be restricted, regardless of their citizenship.

If you have questions about this policy, please contact our compliance team at [compliance@byteful.com](mailto:compliance@byteful.com).
