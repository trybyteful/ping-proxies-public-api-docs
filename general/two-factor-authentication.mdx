---
title: "Two-factor Authentication (2FA)"
description: "Learn how to protect your account with an authenticator app or email codes"
---

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a code each time you log in, after your password. A person who steals your password cannot log in without that code.

Byteful supports two methods. One method is on at a time.

| Method | How it works |
| ------ | ------------ |
| **Authenticator app** (recommended) | You enter a 6-digit code from an app such as Google Authenticator, 1Password or Authy. The code changes every 30 seconds. |
| **Email codes** | We send an 8-digit code to your email address each time you log in. |

<Warning>
2FA applies only when you log in with your email address and password. When you log in with Google, or with another sign-in provider, Byteful does not ask for a 2FA code. To protect those logins, turn on 2-step verification with your sign-in provider, for example in your Google account.
</Warning>

You manage 2FA on the **Security** tab of **User settings**, in the **Two-step authentication** card.

To open it:

1. Open the workspace switcher popup at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Click **User settings**.
3. Click the **Security** tab.

## Setting Up an Authenticator App

Setup has three steps. Keep your phone near you before you start.

### Step 1: Confirm your email address

1. In the **Authenticator app** row, click **Set up**.
2. Click **Send code**. We send an 8-digit code to your email address.
3. Enter the code, then click **Confirm**.

<img
  src="/images/general/two-factor-authentication/setup-confirm-email.png"
  alt="Byteful Confirm Your Email Address"
/>

### Step 2: Connect your authenticator app

1. Open your authenticator app and add a new account.
2. Scan the QR code in the dashboard.
3. Enter the 6-digit code that the app shows, then click **Verify and continue**.

If you cannot scan the QR code, click **Cannot scan it? Enter a key instead**. In your app, choose to enter a setup key, then type the key that the dashboard shows.

<img
  src="/images/general/two-factor-authentication/setup-connect-app.png"
  alt="Byteful Connect Your Authenticator App"
/>

### Step 3: Save your backup code

Your authenticator app is now on. The dashboard shows a backup code. If you lose the device with your authenticator app, the backup code lets you log in once.

1. Click **Copy** or **Download**, and keep the code in a safe place, for example your password manager.
2. Select **I have saved my backup code**.
3. Click **Done**.

<img
  src="/images/general/two-factor-authentication/setup-backup-code.png"
  alt="Byteful Save Your Backup Code"
/>

<Warning>
You see the backup code only once. Save it before you click **Done**.
</Warning>

The **Authenticator app** row now shows **On**.

<img
  src="/images/general/two-factor-authentication/security-card-on.png"
  alt="Byteful Authenticator App On"
/>

## Turning On Email Codes

1. In the **Email codes** row, click **Turn on**.
2. We send an 8-digit code to your email address. Enter the code, then click **Confirm**.

The **Email codes** row now shows **On**.

If you turn on the authenticator app later, email codes turn off.

## Logging In With 2FA

Byteful asks for a code only when you log in with your email address and password. A login with Google does not ask for a code.

1. Enter your email address and password as usual.
2. Enter the code from your authenticator app, or from the email that we send.
3. Click **Submit code**.

<img
  src="/images/general/two-factor-authentication/login-code.png"
  alt="Byteful Two-factor Authentication Login"
/>

### If You Lose Your Device

1. On the code page, click **Lost your device? Use a backup code**.
2. Enter your backup code, then click **Submit code**.

The backup code works once. After you use it, the authenticator app turns off and email codes turn on. Set up your authenticator app again to get a new backup code.

## Confirming Changes With 2FA

Some changes, for example a new password, ask for a 2FA code. When you use an authenticator app, choose **Authenticator app** or **Backup code** at the top of the dialog, then enter the code.

<img
  src="/images/general/two-factor-authentication/confirm-change.png"
  alt="Byteful Confirm a Change With 2FA"
/>

## Turning Off 2FA

1. In the row of the method that is on, click **Turn off**.
2. Click **Disable**.
3. Enter a code to confirm the change.

<Note>
An organization can require 2FA from its members. If you turn off 2FA, you cannot log in to those organizations until you turn it on again. See [Requiring Two-factor Authentication](/general/organizations/requiring-two-factor-authentication).
</Note>
